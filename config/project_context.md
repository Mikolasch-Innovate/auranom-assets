## Research Foundation

**Framework Classification:** AURANOM is a **Conceptual Framework** — a structured theoretical construct developed to explore and define the conditions under which Vertical Multi-Agent AI Systems can create measurable business value in management consulting. It is not a validated product, a proven theory, or a finished architecture. It is the central research object of this DBA dissertation.

**Development Process:** The AURANOM conceptual framework was developed over approximately 3 months through **Human-AI Collaboration** across multiple AI models and platforms (including Claude, Perplexity, Manus, OpenAI, Gemini, Copilot, and others). The sole human participant and decision-maker throughout this process is the HITL researcher. All other roles — researcher, developer, reviewer, consultant, coach — were fulfilled by AI personas operating within defined parameters.

**Maturity Assessment:** An independent review by a Deep Tech research funding body confirmed that AURANOM represents a promising concept, but does not yet meet the empirical depth required for Deep Tech classification. Reviewer feedback: *"In der Regel forschen unsere Bewerber 1–2 Jahre an ihren Themen bevor sie zu uns kommen."* This assessment is acknowledged and directly informs the research design: the DBA dissertation is the structured response to this gap.

**Implication for all agents:**
- Treat AURANOM as a **research hypothesis in framework form** — not as a finished solution.
- Every publication (blog, paper, documentation) is a **research iteration**, contributing evidence that substantiates, challenges, or improves the framework.
- If external evidence contradicts an AURANOM assumption: **flag it explicitly — do not suppress it.**
- Component names and architectural details may be referenced when they directly serve a research argument. Default framing remains at capability level. HITL decides per run.

## AURANOM Framework - 10 Core Components

1.  **DPO - Dual-Process Orchestration**  
    Category: Orchestration / Revenue  
    Framework for bridging the gap between sales and delivery in professional services. A coordinated team of 9 AI agents under an orchestrator ensures that sales promises (ISO 9001) are seamlessly executed during consulting (ISO 20700).

2.  **ACHP - Autonomous Context-Aware Handoff Protocol**  
    Category: Agent Communication  
    Standard communication protocol for autonomous agents. A robust three-stage handshake process with strict quality gates before, during, and after task handoffs. Prevents faulty work and incomplete information.

3.  **CPLS - Confidential & Privacy-Preserving Learning System**  
    Category: Learning & Privacy  
    Solves the enterprise AI dilemma: learning from all customers without sharing secrets. A Federated Learning architecture combined with Zero-Knowledge Proofs aggregates global intelligence while keeping customer data local.

4.  **AURA - Avatar System**  
    Category: User Interface  
    Real-time multimodal avatar system that visualizes the AI's internal 'brain state'. Reacts dynamically to conversation, showing confidence, empathy, or deep thought. Creates a level of trust and rapport impossible with text interfaces.

5.  **ACI - Adaptive Consulting Intelligence**  
    Category: Strategic Intelligence  
    The strategic brain of the autonomous enterprise. Replaces static workflow templates with dynamic process generation. Analyzes 22 project parameters (customer industry, budget, risk) and autonomously designs tailor-made project plans.

6.  **SABEE - Sandbox Execution Engine**  
    Category: Execution Environment  
    Long-term memory and execution environment for AI agents. Overcomes the context window limitation of LLMs with a hierarchical 3-layer memory (RAG³). Enables infinite project history without slowdown or massive API costs.

7.  **FEGR - Facial & Gestic Recognition**  
    Category: Visual Intelligence  
    Visual intelligence for AI agents. Enables agents to 'see' the room during video calls. Analyzes facial expressions
