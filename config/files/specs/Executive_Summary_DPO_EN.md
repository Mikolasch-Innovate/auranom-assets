# Executive Summary: DPO (Dual-Process Orchestration)

**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 29 November 2025
**Module:** Orchestration / Revenue

---

## The Problem

Sales and delivery are separate worlds:
- Promises are lost during handoffs
- Delivery identifies upselling opportunities but cannot pass them back to sales
- Customers experience discontinuity
- Revenue opportunities are missed

**Result:** Customer churn, lost revenue

---

## The Solution: DPO

**Coordinated 9-Agent Team with Bidirectional Handoffs**

**Sales Wing (Agents A–C):**
- Lead Generation → Qualification
- Proposal Writing → Negotiation
- Contract Closing

**Consulting Wing (Agents D–H):**
- Onboarding → Discovery
- Strategy Development
- Implementation & Execution
- QA & Delivery
- Upsell Opportunity Detection

**Master Orchestrator:**
- Manages both wings
- Guarantees context preservation
- Triggers upselling loops

---

## Closed-Loop Revenue Cycle

```
Lead → Qualification → Proposal → Contract
                                      ↓
                           Delivery Success
                                      ↓
                          Upsell Opportunity
                                      ↓
                              New Lead
```
