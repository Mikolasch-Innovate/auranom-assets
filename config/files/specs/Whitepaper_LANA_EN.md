# Whitepaper: Language Analysis System (LANA)

**Version:** 2.0 (Public Version)
**Date:** 29 November 2025
**Classification:** Public / Investor Relations

---

## 1. Executive Summary
LANA is the "ears" of the autonomous enterprise. It goes beyond simple speech-to-text. By fusing text analysis with prosodic audio analysis (tone, pitch, speed), LANA can detect sarcasm, urgency and hidden intent. It translates these human signals into "actionable triggers" that steer AI agents in real time.

## 2. The Challenge
"Great job!" can mean "thank you" or "you have completely failed", depending on the tone. Standard AI reads only the text and misses the sarcasm. This leads to embarrassing errors where bots thank angry customers for their complaints.

## 3. The Solution: Multimodal Listening
LANA listens with two brains:
1.  **Semantic Brain:** Analyses *what* is being said (text).
2.  **Prosodic Brain:** Analyses *how* it is being said (audio features).

A dedicated **Fusion Logic** compares the two. If the text is positive but the tone is aggressive, LANA flags "Sarcasm" and instructs the agent to de-escalate.

## 4. Key Innovations
*   **Sarcasm Detection:** The ability to resolve conflicts between words and voice.
*   **Actionable Triggers:** LANA does not simply output "Anger: 80%". It issues a command: "ESCALATE_TO_HUMAN, URGENCY_HIGH, ADOPT_APOLOGETIC_TONE".
*   **Input Guard Integration:** LANA proactively filters "jailbreak" attempts spoken by users before they reach the LLM.

## 5. Market Differentiation
| Feature | Standard Speech-to-Text / NLP | LANA Analysis Engine |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Analysis Depth** | Text only | Text + audio prosody |
| **Sarcasm** | Blind | Robust detection |
| **Output** | Transcript | System command (trigger) |

## 6. Use Cases
*   **Customer Support:** Identifying frustrated callers *before* they escalate.
*   **Negotiation Bot:** Detecting when a counterpart hesitates or bluffs based on voice tremors.
