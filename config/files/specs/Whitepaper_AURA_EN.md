# Whitepaper: AURA Avatar System

**Version:** 2.0 (Public Version)
**Date:** 29 November 2025
**Classification:** Public / Investor Relations

---

## 1. Executive Summary
AURA gives AI a face. It is a real-time multimodal avatar system that visualises the internal "brain state" of the AI. Unlike scripted video bots, AURA responds dynamically to conversation, displaying confidence, empathy or deep contemplation — creating a level of trust and rapport that is impossible with text interfaces.

## 2. The Challenge
Text-based AI is efficient but impersonal. In high-value negotiations or coaching sessions, human connection is key. Existing avatars are either pre-rendered videos (slow, non-interactive) or simple puppets (robotic). They lack the subtle non-verbal cues that make communication feel "real".

## 3. The Solution: State-Driven Animation
AURA connects the internal logic of the AI directly to a high-resolution 3D face (Unreal Engine MetaHuman).
*   **Confidence:** When the AI is 99% certain, AURA maintains eye contact. When uncertain, it may look away.
*   **Empathy:** When the user is sad (detected by FEGR), AURA's expression softens.
*   **Processing:** When complex data is being calculated, AURA displays "thinking" gestures instead of freezing.

## 4. Key Innovations
*   **Live Business Coupling:** Avatar behaviour is driven by business logic (sales stage, negotiation hardness), not random movements.
*   **Handoff Morphing:** When the system switches from a "Sales Agent" to a "Technical Expert", the avatar visually transforms (clothing, voice, demeanour) to immediately signal the role change.
*   **Sub-200ms Latency:** Specifically optimised for real-time conversation.

## 5. Market Differentiation
| Feature | Video Gen AI (Synthesia, HeyGen) | AURA System |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Interactivity** | None (Pre-recorded) | Fully real-time |
| **Driver** | Text input | AI internal state & emotion |
| **Latency** | Minutes (rendering) | Milliseconds |

## 6. Use Cases
*   **Digital Sales Rep:** Reading the room and adjusting expressions to close a deal.
*   **Executive Coach:** Providing empathetic, face-to-face feedback on leadership skills.
