# Executive Summary: AURA (Avatar System)

**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 29 November 2025
**Module:** Interaction / UX

---

## The Problem

Text AI is cold. Pre-recorded video avatars are robotic:
- No emotional connection
- No subtle non-verbal communication
- No trust in high-value deals
- Video AI tools feel "fake"

**Result:** Low conversion rates, poor rapport

---

## The Solution: AURA

**Real-time Avatar with AI-State-Driven Animation**

**State-to-Animation Mapping:**

| AI State | Avatar Shows |
|----------|-------------|
| Confidence 95% | Direct Eye Contact, Open Posture |
| Confidence 50% | Gaze Down, Hesitant Gestures |
| Empathy Detected | Softened Eyes, Slight Head Tilt |
| Processing | Hand to Chin, Thinking Expression |
| Handoff | Clothing/Voice Transform |

**Handoff Morphing Example:**
```
Sales Agent → Consulting Agent:
- Suit: Blue → Business Casual
- Posture: Selling → Collaborative
- Voice: Upbeat → Professional
- Energy: Dynamic → Deliberate
```

---

## Technical Features

- **Photorealistic Avatar:** Unreal Engine 5 MetaHuman
- **Sub-200ms Latency:** Audio to animation
- **Real-time Streaming:** WebRTC to browser
- **State-Driven:** Behaviour driven by business logic

## Innovation

- **Live Business Coupling:** Avatar responds to sales stage, negotiation hardness
- **Authentic Interaction:** Not pre-recorded, truly real-time
