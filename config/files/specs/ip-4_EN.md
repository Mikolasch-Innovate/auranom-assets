# Technical Specification: AURA Avatar System

## 1. Title of the Invention

**System and Method for the Generation and Control of a Multimodal, AI-Driven Avatar for Human–Agent Interaction**

**Short Title:** AURA Avatar System

---

## 2. Problem Description

The interaction between humans and AI agents is often text-based and impersonal. This limits the bandwidth of communication and prevents the development of trust and a natural interaction dynamic, particularly in complex business environments.

---

## 3. Solution: The AURA Avatar System Architecture

The AURA Avatar System solves this problem by providing a multimodal, AI-driven avatar that serves as the interface between the human user and the multi-agent system.

### 3.1. Multimodal Interaction

The avatar can communicate via multiple channels:

- **Speech:** Text-to-Speech (TTS) and Speech-to-Text (STT)
- **Facial Expression:** Lip synchronisation and emotional facial expressions
- **Gestures:** Body posture and hand gestures

### 3.2. AI-Driven Control

The avatar is controlled in real time by the AI agents of the Auranom system. The actions and reactions of the avatar are based on the internal states and decisions of the agents.

---

## 4. Patentable Core Features

1.  **Real-Time Control of the Avatar by a Multi-Agent System:** The direct coupling of avatar animation to the internal states of a complex AI system.
2.  **Multimodal Synchronisation:** The simultaneous and consistent control of the avatar's speech, facial expression and gestures.
3.  **Emotional Representation:** The avatar's ability to visually represent emotions detected or generated by the system.

---

## 5. Distinction from the State of the Art

| Technology/Work | Nearest Relevant State of the Art | Distinction and Novelty of the AURA Avatar System |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Virtual Assistants** | **Apple Siri, Amazon Alexa** | These are voice-based and have no visual, multimodal representation. |
| **Gaming Avatars** | **Unreal Engine Metahumans** [1] | Gaming avatars are controlled by human players. The AURA avatar is controlled by an **autonomous multi-agent system**. |
| **VTuber Technology** | **Live2D, VSeeFace** | VTuber avatars are controlled through human motion capture. The AURA avatar is **algorithmically controlled by AI agents**. |
