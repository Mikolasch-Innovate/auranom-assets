# Executive Summary: G-EE (Governance & Execution Engine)

**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 29 November 2025
**Module:** Foundation / Governance

---

## The Problem

Autonomous AI agents are a black box:
- Can hallucinate and make false promises
- Can leak PII
- Can exceed budgets
- Traditional monitoring reports errors *after* they occur

**Risk:** Uncontrolled, potentially harmful agent actions

---

## The Solution: G-EE

**Real-time Governance Engine with Pre-emptive Enforcement**

**Dual Governance Model:**
1. **System Governance** (immutable)
   - Hard limits: No PII, ISO 27001 compliance
   - Applies to all projects and clients

2. **Project Governance** (configurable)
   - Custom rules: budget limits, tone requirements
   - Adjustable per project

**10 ARGUS Services** validate every agent action:
- Budget Guard
- Privacy Shield
- Compliance Validator
- Input Guard (Anti-Prompt-Injection)
- Loop Detector
- Resource Monitor
- Sentiment Watch
- Audit Logger
- Kill Switch
- + 1 more
