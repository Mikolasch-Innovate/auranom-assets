# Whitepaper: Dual-Process Orchestration (DPO)

**Date:** 29 November 2025
**Classification:** Public / Investor Relations

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Dual-Process Orchestration (DPO) is a specialised framework for bridging the gap between sales and delivery in professional services. By deploying a coordinated team of 9 AI agents under a single orchestrator, DPO ensures that the promises made during the "Sales" phase (ISO 9001) are seamlessly executed during consulting (ISO 20700) and that new upselling opportunities are also identified.

## 2. The Challenge
In many organisations, sales and delivery operate in silos. Sales teams close deals with specific promises that are often lost or misunderstood when handed over to delivery teams. Conversely, delivery teams identify new client needs that could generate new revenue, but have no channel to report this back to sales. This leads to client dissatisfaction and lost revenue.

## 3. The Solution: Integrated Agent Teams
DPO deploys a unified squad of AI agents structured in two wings:
*   **Sales Wing (Agents A–C):** Handles lead generation, proposal writing and contract management.
*   **Consulting Wing (Agents D–H):** Handles strategy, implementation and quality assurance.

A **Master Orchestrator** manages the flow between these wings, ensuring that data and context are fully preserved during the handoff from sales to consulting — and back.

## 4. Key Innovations
1.  **Context-Aware Handoffs:** Automated transfer of the complete client context (expectations, tone, constraints) from sales to delivery agents, eliminating the "starting from scratch" feeling for clients.
2.  **Automated Upselling:** Delivery agents are trained to identify opportunities for additional value. These are automatically packaged as "leads" and passed back to the sales agents, creating a flywheel effect.
3.  **Dual ISO Compliance:** The system technically enforces ISO 9001 (quality management) for sales steps and ISO 20700 (guidelines for management consulting) for delivery steps.

## 5. Market Differentiation
| Feature | Standalone CRM / Sales AI | DPO System |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Focus** | Closing deals | Delivering value & closing deals |
| **Integration** | Often requires manual data entry | Native, agent-to-agent context flow |
| **Revenue Cycle** | Linear (lead → closed won) | Circular (sale → delivery → upsell) |

## 6. Use Cases
*   **Digital Agencies:** Automating the complete client lifecycle from "hello" to final report and renewal.
*   **SaaS Onboarding:** Ensuring that the special-case use cases discussed in sales are actually configured during technical onboarding.
