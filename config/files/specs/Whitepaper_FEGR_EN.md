# Whitepaper: Facial & Gestic Recognition (FEGR)

**Version:** 2.0 (Public Version)
**Date:** 29 November 2025
**Classification:** Public / Investor Relations

---

## 1. Executive Summary
FEGR is the visual intelligence for AI agents. It enables agents to "see" the room during video calls. By analysing facial expressions, gestures (such as hand-raising) and group engagement levels, FEGR agents can moderate meetings and respond to non-verbal cues like a human facilitator.

## 2. The Challenge
Video calls are the standard for business, but AI agents are visually blind. They cannot see who is speaking, who is bored, or who wants to raise their hand to speak. This makes them poor moderators and disconnects them from group dynamics.

## 3. The Solution: Seminar Room Intelligence
FEGR uses privacy-preserving computer vision to track:
1.  **Individual Signals:** Emotions (confusion? joy?) and gestures (thumbs up?).
2.  **Group Dynamics:** Are people paying attention or looking at their phones?
3.  **Speaker Identity:** Who is currently speaking? (Without storing biometric databases.)

## 4. Key Innovations
*   **"Raise Hand" Trigger:** FEGR detects the specific hand-raising gesture and immediately pauses the agent to allow the human to speak.
*   **Privacy-First Tracking:** Uses temporary session IDs to track participants ("Person A", "Person B") without storing facial recognition data, fully GDPR-compliant.
*   **Group Engagement Score:** Aggregates individual attention metrics to warn the agent when it is losing the room ("Boredom Alert").

## 5. Market Differentiation
| Feature | Standard Face Recognition APIs | FEGR Visual Engine |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Focus** | Identity / security | Interaction / moderation |
| **Privacy** | Biometric database | Anonymous session IDs |
| **Output** | "That is John" | "John wants to speak" |

## 6. Use Cases
*   **AI Workshop Facilitator:** An agent managing a brainstorming session and ensuring everyone gets a chance to speak.
*   **Sales Presentation:** Detecting when a prospect looks confused and automatically pausing to ask "Shall I clarify this point?"
