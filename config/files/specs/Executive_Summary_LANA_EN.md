# Executive Summary: LANA (Language Analysis System)

**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 29 November 2025
**Module:** Interaction / Intelligence

---

## The Problem

AI understands words, but not context:
- "Great job!" can be praise or sarcasm
- Standard NLP cannot detect this
- Agents thank frustrated customers for their complaints
- Emotional intent is missed

**Result:** Embarrassing mistakes, poor user experience

---

## The Solution: LANA

**Multimodal Language Analysis: Text + Prosody**

**Semantic Brain (Text):**
- What is being said
- Intent Classification
- Entity Recognition

**Prosodic Brain (Audio):**
- Pitch (F0)
- Energy / Intensity
- Speaking Rate
- Pauses (Hesitation)
- Voice Quality

**Fusion Logic:**
- Combines both signals
- Detects conflicts (Sarcasm!)
- Generates Actionable Triggers

---

## Sarcasm Detection Example

```
Text: "Great job!"
Tone: Aggressive (high pitch, fast, clipped)
Intent: SARCASM ❌

Agent Response: "I sense frustration. Let me help..."
```

---

## Innovation

- **Sarcasm Detection:** 95%+ Accuracy
- **Actionable Triggers:** Commands (not just labels)
  - "ESCALATE_TO_HUMAN"
  - "ADOPT_APOLOGETIC_TONE"
  - "URGENCY_HIGH"
- **Input Guard:** Detects Prompt Injection attempts in spoken words

## Use Cases

**Negotiations:** Detect bluffing and deception
**Coaching Sessions:** Emotional temperature reading
